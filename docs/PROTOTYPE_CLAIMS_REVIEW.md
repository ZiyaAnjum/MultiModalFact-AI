# Prototype Claims Review Document (`docs/PROTOTYPE_CLAIMS_REVIEW.md`)

> **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER**: The 45 claims in this prototype dataset were **hand-authored and visually reviewed** against the 15 downloaded MS-COCO images, **not model-generated**. This dataset serves strictly as an end-to-end **pipeline prototype**, not a benchmark or evaluation standard.

---

## 1. Prototype Overview

- **Source Dataset**: MS-COCO 2014 (`yerevann/coco-karpathy` test split annotations).
- **Source Images**: 15 authentic JPEGs located in `data/raw/images/`.
- **Generation Method**: Zero-paid-API, offline cache-backed generation via `src/data/generator.py` (`build_synthetic_dataset`).
- **Total Samples**: 45 rows in `data/processed/dataset.csv`.
- **Label Distribution**: Exactly balanced (15 `consistent`, 15 `contradictory`, 15 `insufficient`).
- **Image-Level Partitioning**: Seed 42, zero image leakage across splits (10 train / 2 val / 3 test images; 30 train / 6 val / 9 test samples).
